# Ellie the Elephant

Ellie is the friendly High-Tech Steward for Singapore American School (SAS). She is designed to help students understand the health and performance of their school buildings through emotion, movement, color, and recognizable accessories.

## Mascot concept

Ellie combines an emotionally approachable elephant with a subtle digital layer. She is not intended to look like a robot. Instead, she feels like a soft animated character who is naturally connected to the building’s digital nervous system: its Niagara Building Management System, environmental sensors, energy meters, and operational data.

Her role is to translate technical building information into a form children can understand at a glance. As the concept develops, students should be able to infer whether the building is healthy, conserving energy, experiencing a problem, or celebrating an improvement by looking at Ellie’s expression, posture, ear color, trunk glow, clothing, and symbols.

## Visual design and meaning

| Feature | Design | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Soft slate-blue elephant with rounded, compact proportions | Slate blue communicates calmness, intelligence, trust, and a connection to SAS blue. The plush proportions keep Ellie welcoming rather than technical or intimidating. |
| Eyes | Large, expressive animated-family-film eyes with glossy highlights and small heart-shaped catchlights | Her eyes are the main emotional interface. They make Ellie approachable and can communicate happiness, concern, curiosity, or appreciation. The heart highlights reinforce care for students and the campus. |
| Ears | Large, expressive ears with a restrained cyan edge glow | Ellie is always “listening” to the building. The glow represents live awareness of environmental conditions and can eventually change or pulse to communicate building health. |
| Data trunk | Gently upturned trunk with a few smooth rings and a soft cyan fiber-optic light at the tip | The trunk acts as a friendly sensor. Ellie can “sniff” air quality, feel temperature, and connect to classroom sensors and building data. |
| Technology accent | Soft cyan used consistently on the ear edges, trunk rings, and trunk-tip sparkle | A single accent color creates one clear visual language for technology. It avoids exposed machinery, cables, chrome, armor, or competing dashboard-like details. |
| Vest | Cozy SAS-red and navy steward vest with crisp white piping | The vest ties Ellie to the school’s red, white, and blue identity while making her look helpful and ready to support the campus community. |
| Eagle patch | Small white eagle silhouette on the navy pocket | The patch connects Ellie to the SAS Eagles. It establishes her as part of the school without turning her into a replacement for the existing eagle identity. |
| Sensor pen | Small device carried in the vest pocket | Suggests observation, measurement, and connection to building sensors without making Ellie appear mechanical. |
| Safety helmet | Miniature yellow helmet clipped at the hip as an occasional facilities-role accessory | Represents safety, maintenance, and practical stewardship. Yellow is intentionally used only as a small warm accent. |
| Cheeks and smile | Soft warm blush, gentle eyebrows, and a small joyful smile | These details balance the cool blue palette and make Ellie feel kind, attentive, and emotionally lovable. |
| Surface style | Premium stylized 3D clay-toy finish with velvety matte surfaces | The soft material treatment matches the playful typography and avoids the uncanny feeling of a photorealistic or hard robotic mascot. |
| Grounding shadow | Soft ellipse beneath Ellie’s feet | Gives the character physical presence and prevents her from appearing pasted onto the background. |
| Background | Warm white or very pale blue studio field with minimal data accents | Keeps attention on Ellie and allows her to sit naturally within a clean school website without a visible rectangular backdrop seam. |

## Building-health communication

Ellie is intended to become a visual language for the living campus:

- **Healthy and comfortable:** relaxed posture, warm smile, bright eyes, and a calm cyan ear glow.
- **Energy-saving mode:** cool, gently pulsing ears and a peaceful or focused expression.
- **Needs attention:** concerned expression, lowered posture, and a more noticeable alert symbol or role accessory.
- **Investigating:** thinking or listening gesture, with the data trunk directed toward the source of information.
- **Positive improvement:** cheering, clapping, or celebrating when students reduce energy use or improve environmental performance.
- **Resting or low activity:** a calm sleeping pose that can represent quiet hours or reduced building demand.

The emotional signal should always be understandable before any numerical data is shown. Color and symbols support the expression; they should not replace it.

## Gesture set

Ellie currently cycles automatically through nine character actions:

1. **Wave** — a warm greeting and introduction.
2. **Cheer** — encouragement and positive feedback.
3. **Celebrate** — recognition of a shared achievement.
4. **Think** — curiosity, analysis, or investigation.
5. **Listen** — attention to students or live sensor information.
6. **Rest** — quiet time, low activity, or a gentle pause.
7. **Clap** — support for student participation and progress.
8. **Peekaboo** — playfulness and discovery.
9. **Bow** — gratitude, politeness, and a friendly goodbye.

The gestures use the same character identity, colors, clothing, proportions, and technology details so Ellie feels like one living mascot rather than a collection of unrelated renders.

## Recorded image-generation prompt

The following prompt records the approved direction used to create the refined Ellie mascot concept, normalized only to use her final public name, **Ellie**. Later feedback removed the circular background element.

```text
Asset type: Cute mascot concept for the Singapore American School website

Input image: Edit the immediately previous Ellie the Elephant concept. Preserve her identity, SAS-inspired colors, health-responsive ears, glowing data trunk, red/navy/white vest, eagle pocket patch, and clipped yellow safety helmet.

Primary request: Make Ellie substantially cuter, sweeter, and more emotionally lovable without making her look infantile or losing her High-Tech Steward role.

Character changes: Increase head size by about 20%; make the body shorter and rounder with plush compact proportions; shorten arms and legs; enlarge the eyes slightly with glossy warm highlights and tiny heart-shaped digital catchlights; add round soft cheeks with a faint warm blush; make eyebrows softer and more expressive; give her a small closed-mouth joyful smile; tilt the head gently; curve the trunk upward in a friendly hello; make the ears rounder and slightly more floppy. Pose should feel bashful and welcoming, with one small forefoot raised and the other close to the body.

Technology treatment: Keep technology subtle and magical — only a few smooth trunk rings, a tiny soft cyan fiber-optic sparkle at the tip, and delicate cyan ear-edge glows (single accent color only, no separate green — this keeps the “tech glow” reading as one coherent signature rather than two competing hues). Remove any remaining hard robotic or armored feeling.

Clothing: Retain the SAS-red and navy vest with white piping and white eagle silhouette pocket patch, but make it softer, smaller, and more like a cozy school steward vest; simplify fasteners; keep one tiny sensor pen and the miniature yellow safety helmet at the hip.

Style/medium: Premium stylized 3D clay-toy mascot illustration, velvety matte surfaces, rounded shapes, charming animated-family-film appeal, polished enough for a premium school website.

Composition: Square, full body centered, generous padding around ears, trunk, and feet; soft elliptical grounding shadow.

Backdrop: Warm white studio background with a very faint navy-to-cyan gradient data arc and sparse tiny sensor dots, so the background glow matches the character’s accent color rather than introducing a separate tone.

Palette: Dominant slate-blue body (about 60% of the color weight); SAS navy and school red as the vest’s secondary block (about 30%); crisp white as the connecting neutral for piping, background, and catchlights. All technology elements share a single signature accent — soft cyan only — used sparingly on trunk rings, ear-edge glow, and sparkle. Yellow appears only once, on the miniature helmet, as a small warm accent echoing the blush and warm backdrop.

Color harmony: Warm skin and cheek tones and the warm white backdrop should dominate the overall feel; cool cyan stays a minority accent and should never compete with navy and red for visual weight.

Constraints: Change cuteness and proportions while preserving all listed identity features; one character only; no text; no watermark; no extra logo; no armor; no chrome; no exposed mechanics; no dense dashboards; no dangling cables; no cropped ears, trunk, or feet.
```

## Naming and usage rules

- The mascot’s public name is **Ellie**. “Ele” may remain in internal legacy route names or asset identifiers, but it should not appear as her displayed name.
- Keep SAS red, navy, and white as the main clothing colors.
- Reserve cyan for technology and building-awareness signals.
- Keep Ellie emotionally expressive and easy for children to understand.
- Avoid photorealism, hard robotics, exposed machinery, military styling, dense interfaces, or visual clutter.
- Preserve sufficient space around her ears, trunk, and feet in every composition.
